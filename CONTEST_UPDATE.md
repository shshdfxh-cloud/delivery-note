## Revised concept

A general-purpose delivery audit driven by the customer's intended outcome. Instead of trusting a polished completion message, it connects the customer objective to actual materials, structured criteria, exact source quotations, independently measured checks and a report of contradictions and missing evidence. Website testing is one adapter, not the product's definition.

## What judges can run

1. Open the same demo URL and click the recorded SERV example. The supplied synthetic training handoff says all 12 people completed; actual CSV parsing finds 10 completed and 2 pending. The real recorded SERV response also finds missing follow-up arrangements and unsupported execution/acceptance claims. Fresh byte, quote and bounded data checks run on every replay. No key or account required.
2. Select your own UTF-8 materials and objective. Inspect the prompt, choose whether to send it to SERV, return the complete structured response, and inspect its precise quotations, actual checks, limitations and downloadable JSON/Markdown. The public static page does not make hidden live model calls.
3. Open checks.html for source-data recomputation (including a same-row-count monetary corruption) and the existing supported offline browser journeys. Static replay never pretends that runtime execution occurred.

The local service additionally offers opt-in full-material API review and an existing-assistant browser handoff; see README.md and AGENT_REVIEW.md for transport requirements and validation limits.

## SERV contribution and evidence

SERV reasons over the customer's goal and the supplied evidence to expose contradictions, omitted conditions and missing verification, and may select bounded checks. The engine validates exact references and runs those checks independently. Real recorded output and its independent validation are in verification/serv-goal-tools-20260922.json and verification/serv-goal-tools-validated.json. A browser-observed response is not an authenticated API receipt.

## Intended use and limits

The intended users are freelance builders, small studios and clients reviewing software/data/report handoffs. Reduced unsupported completion claims is the intended benefit; willingness to pay and revenue are hypotheses, not proven customers or income. We claim no awards. This is not a universal security audit, real-world source authentication or automatic customer-acceptance certificate.
